About

I lead backend architecture for Samsung's device-financing lending platform, live in Thailand, Indonesia, the Philippines and India. The work is real-time payment reconciliation, loan-lifecycle correctness and making each new market a configuration change rather than a code fork.

Before Samsung, I led the move from batch to streaming fraud detection at Western Union, and spent nearly five years at Cisco building entitlement and order platforms, including a monolith-to-microservices migration and a Neo4j-to-Neptune move. Across all of it, I care most about the problems where correctness matters more than throughput, and I write about the tradeoffs below.
