Bitemporal loan servicing engine In progress

Tackles the hardest problem in lending software: a payment dated 3 March that arrives on 20 March. The design event-sources loans on two time axes (when it happened, when we learned it), so backdated payments, reversals and rate changes recompute interest, fees and delinquency deterministically, and any past view stays reproducible for audit.

High-contention double-entry ledger In progress

Targets strictly serializable transfers when a few hot accounts take most of the traffic. The design uses single-writer partitions with batched commit instead of row locks, and exactly-once transfers under client retries. Verification is planned through deterministic simulation testing (seeded crashes, torn writes, reordering) and checked histories.
